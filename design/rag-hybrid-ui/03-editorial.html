Q

What does RRF stand for and how does it fuse the two ranked lists?

A

RRF stands for Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Each document earns a contribution of 1 / (k + rank) from every list it appears in, where k is a damping constant — typically 60. A document ranked high in both lists accumulates the most score, and no score normalization is needed, because RRF only uses ranks.[1]
